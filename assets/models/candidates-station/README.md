families as the models already in Night Express (KayKit and Tiny Treats by Kay and Isa Lousberg, Quaternius, Kenney).
Every pack here is CC0; the licence file of each pack sits in its folder. Nothing here is in the game yet: once the
owner picks, the chosen pieces go through `scripts/assets/bake_models.py` like the session 49–54 models.

`renders/` holds the page's pictures (each option on the real platform at night, and today's pieces).

Paid packs shown on the page only (not bought): Tiny Treats Bakery Building ($7.95, recommended: a modular shopfront
for the booking office and station house), Tiny Treats Lovely Living Room ($7.95, the only clock in our style),
Quaternius Medieval Village MegaKit Pro ($9.99), KayKit City Builder Bits Extra ($3.95).

Not found free in this style: a station clock, a sack trolley, suitcases, bicycles, hay bales, cloth bunting, rope
stanchions.

Rendering: `scripts/assets/station-picks-stage.ts` is the picks stage on the station platform (the game's own
`PlatformView` with its Phase 2 upgrades, the train beside it, the night lighting and camera); build it with
`ENTRY=scripts/assets/station-picks-stage.ts OUT=station-picks-stage.html node scripts/build.mjs` on a checkout that
has the Phase 2 platform (`PlatformView.setUpgrades`, branch `claude/new-session-zlmzpt`). `station-picks-shoot.mjs`
shoots the specs; `scripts/assets/station-picks/` holds the downloader (`itch.py`, free itch.io pages), the catalogue
(`catalog.py`), the crops (`crop.py`), the layout sketch (`layout.py`), the page (`page.py`) and `collect.py`, which
copied the files here. They take the scratch folder of the session that made them as their argument.
