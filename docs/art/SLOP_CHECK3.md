portrait, every 6 s, the autopilot playing, cards answered after 3 s) on the build with Stage B, and was asked to list

**Their verdict, in short:** a cosy toy diorama that still reads as a well-organised prototype. The camera wastes most of
the portrait screen, large surfaces the camera dwells on (tender, lobby, rear deck, café) are untextured, every sign
comes from a different kit, repetition is everywhere and night is a colour grade rather than lamplight. Fix first: the
framing and a dressed foreground; one marker language with nothing over the player, the HUD or the edge; the grey
particles and the blockout surfaces.
